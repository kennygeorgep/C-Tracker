Privacy and legal. Sharing is opt-in and visible: each person starts and stops it themselves, and a banner shows while it is on. Only the latest position is stored, and it is deleted when you stop. Tracking someone without their knowledge is stalking or a privacy violation in most countries (for example India's BNS stalking provisions, the IT Act and the DPDP Act 2023, and the EU GDPR). Employers need written consent and a clear policy. This is general information, not legal advice.
